Questions and answers

The details, answered.

What is route optimization?

It is the work of sequencing every stop into the fewest, fastest routes against your real trucks and time windows. Route Planner does it in one pass, then reroutes the moment capacity changes.

Can Curri handle multi-stop and multi-driver routes?

Yes. Route Planner sequences many stops across many drivers at once, balancing the load across your own fleet and, when it runs out, Curri's network.

What vehicle types are supported?

Car, pickup, cargo van, box truck, hotshot trailer, flatbed, LTL, and parcel. The planner matches the right vehicle to each payload.

What happens when my own trucks run out?

The overflow does not sit in an unassigned pile. You book it into Curri's nationwide network from the same board, and it is absorbed by Hotshots and 85+ LTL carriers. You pay only for what you book.

How do I optimize fleet routing and dispatch?

Import or sync your orders, and Route Planner sequences them against your live fleet capacity, assigns each stop to a driver, and dispatches in one pass. When your own trucks fill up, it books the overflow into Curri's network.

Will this help with last-mile delivery?

Yes. Route Planner is built for construction and industrial last-mile: multi-stop routes, live tracking, proof of delivery, and same-day hotshots when a job needs one.

Does your routing software work for couriers?

Yes. Whether the driver is your own, a third-party carrier, or a Curri network driver, you plan, assign, and track every one of them from the same board.

05Questions and answers

The details, answered.

What is a hotshot?

A hotshot is a fast, single-load delivery moved on its own vehicle right now, instead of waiting for the next scheduled run. It is how construction and industrial teams keep a job moving when a part cannot wait.

How fast is hotshot delivery?

Most on-demand jobs are completed same day, often within a couple of hours, depending on distance and the vehicle you pick.

How do I get same-day delivery for building materials?

Set the route, pick a vehicle from cars to stakebeds, and schedule the pickup. Curri matches a local driver and shares live tracking from pickup to drop-off.

What vehicles can I book?

Everything from sedans and SUVs to cargo vans, box trucks, flatbeds, dry vans, and stakebeds, matched to the payload.

Core Intelligence

Eight agents  ·  one core
01The agents

Meet the agents working by the nanosecond for you.

Each agent is tuned to one job in dispatch. They orbit the same core, pull from the same data, and bond into a single system that covers the whole flow.

01Payload Intelligence

Reads every order down to the part, filling in accurate weights and dimensions the moment it comes together, then works out what packs, stacks, and rotates. The shipment is known before it reaches dispatch.

02Smart Import

Drop in a purchase order, a screenshot, or a photo of a ticket and get a finished, ready-to-dispatch draft back. It reads PDFs, images, and pasted text, pulling pickup, drop-off, contacts, and line items on its own.

03Exceptions Agent

Watches every active delivery in real time and catches trouble early, then rebooks with the best available carrier and rate on its own. It pulls a human in only when a situation truly needs one.

04Maya

The always-on copilot that lives in the tools your team and drivers already use. She answers across text, email, voice, and in-app, clearing repeat questions and pulling real shipment status on demand.

05Booking Agent

Text a dedicated line or email an address and get an instant quote back, no portal, no login. It recommends the right vehicle and sends a real price with a quick-book link.

06Coworker

Plans and builds routes inside Route Planner, then runs Smart Dispatch to auto-assign orders where they fit. It manages drivers and vehicles and reoptimizes routes when the day shifts.

07Negotiator

Handles the whole driver back-and-forth at scale, sending and managing offers across many drivers at once and tracking who is in and at what rate as replies come back.

08Compliance Validator

Checks the photo or input a driver submits at each stop against what the job requires. Running in Route Planner, it passes clean deliveries through and flags the ones that do not.
